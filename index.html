How to use this demo

  • 1. Eager Load: The traditional way. Downloads the entire container image before starting, even though most files aren't used.
  • 2. Lazy Load: Our new way. Starts instantly! Only downloads files exactly when the application actually needs them.
  • 3. Warm Start: Running again or sharing files. No downloads needed because data is already locally cached.
